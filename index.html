I'm a

As a beginner in the world of computer science, one area that captivates me the most is Frontend Development, especially the UI/UX design aspect. The ability to craft visually engaging and user-friendly web interfaces excites me because it blends technology with creativity a combination I deeply enjoy. Seeing a user interface take shape from an idea to an interactive experience on the screen is incredibly fulfilling.

I'm currently building my foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which are essential tools for structuring, styling, and adding interactivity to web pages. Alongside these core technologies, I'm also exploring design systems and beginner friendly frameworks like Bootstrap to understand responsive design better and enhance the user experience across devices.

What truly drives my interest is design thinking understanding user's needs, creating intuitive layouts, and choosing the right color palettes, typography, and spacing to build interfaces that are not only functional but also delightful to use. I find joy in tools like Figma and Canva, which allow me to experiment with layout, design prototypes, and visualize how users will interact with a product.

Even though I'm just starting out, I see Frontend Development and UI/UX Design as powerful skills that will allow me to bring meaningful digital experiences to life. Whether it's a personal portfolio, a mobile-friendly e-commerce page, or a user centered web application, I'm excited to keep learning, designing, and building.
